# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include being respectful, welcoming differing viewpoints, giving constructive feedback, and accepting responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, publishing private information, or other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through GitHub's repository contact options. Maintainers will review reports promptly and may remove content, issue warnings, or restrict participation as appropriate. Retaliation against a reporter is not tolerated.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
